Programs

Programs from Red FCU

Red FCU is preparing a set of verification, protection, assistance, and hosting programs for creators, communities, and their projects. Verified Creator and Verified Community are now open for applications. The other programs are still being prepared and cannot be applied for yet.

Verified Creator and Verified Community open Other programs are listed soon or closed; each card says which.

Before you read further

Two programs are open

RedFCU Verified Creator and RedFCU Verified Community are open for applications. Filling out either form creates a JSON file in your browser; you send that file to staff, who review it manually. All other programs on this page are planned and cannot be applied for yet. Nothing on this page is legal advice.

Open for applications

RedFCU Verified Creator

Red FCU reviews submitted evidence to verify that an individual, team, studio, or organization is the legitimate owner or authorized representative of a project.

Benefits

  • Verified Creator badge
  • A verification ID you can quote
  • Eligibility for additional Red FCU programs

Notice. Verification represents the results of Red FCU's internal review process and is not a legal determination of ownership. Submitting an application does not guarantee verification.

RedFCU Verified Project

Red FCU reviews project information and available evidence to verify that a project is authentic and controlled by its verified creator.

Benefits

  • Verified Project badge
  • Official project listing
  • Authentication assistance

Notice. Verification does not constitute a legal certification and is provided solely as a project authenticity review.

Open for applications

RedFCU Verified Community

Red FCU reviews submitted information to confirm that an online community is run by its owners or by an authorized representative, and records the result as a public verification status. The program is intended for eligible communities on the platforms listed below.

Platforms in scope

  • Discord
  • X
  • Yapin
  • Reddit
  • YouTube
  • Facebook
  • Other platforms on request

What it provides

  • Independent verification for eligible online communities
  • RedFCU-issued verification badge
  • A verification ID you can quote
  • Verification status recorded by Red FCU
  • Ownership or authorized representative review

Notice. This is not platform-issued verification. A RedFCU Verified Community badge is issued by Red FCU alone. It is not a checkmark, badge, or endorsement from Discord, X, Yapin, Reddit, YouTube, Facebook, or any other platform, and it does not imply affiliation with any of them. Red FCU may withdraw a verification if the information changes or the badge is misused. A badge that has already been sent cannot be deleted; withdrawal ends the permission to use it. Red FCU does not publish a public directory of verified communities; a verification ID can be confirmed with staff.

RedFCU Protected

Red FCU maintains supporting ownership records and documentation submitted by verified creators to assist with demonstrating ownership when disputes arise.

Benefits

  • Ownership documentation review
  • Evidence organization
  • Protection status badge
  • Priority assistance

Notice. Protected status does not guarantee legal protection or legal outcomes.

Listed soon available

RedFCU Copyright Assistance

Red FCU provides informational and administrative assistance for creators who believe their work has been copied, redistributed, impersonated, or otherwise misused.

Assistance May Include

  • Evidence review
  • Documentation assistance
  • DMCA template preparation
  • Reporting guidance
  • Ownership record organization

Notice. Red FCU is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal representation.

Closed until further notice

RedFCU Community Hosting

A planned managed hosting environment for approved Discord bots, software applications, and community projects supported by Red FCU. The service is still under development while infrastructure, policies, review procedures, and security systems are finalized, so no applications are being accepted.

Planned features

  • Hosting for approved Discord bots
  • Hosting for approved software projects
  • Security and manual review
  • Limited beta availability

Notice. Applications are not being accepted. Capacity, launch date, eligibility requirements, uptime, and final policies have not been announced. Approval, if the program opens, would not guarantee ongoing hosting.

RedFCU Recommended

Projects that have been reviewed by Red FCU and found to meet quality, authenticity, and maintenance standards may receive the Recommended designation.

Benefits

  • Recommendation badge
  • Featured placement opportunities
  • Community recognition

Notice. A recommendation is an internal Red FCU designation. It is not a certification, a statement about legality, or a guarantee of quality.
