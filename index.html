Mental Health Awareness

Your life matters. You are not alone.

If you're experiencing depression, anxiety, loneliness, emotional distress, or thoughts of suicide, support is available. You deserve help, wherever you are in the world.

These services can offer someone to listen, emotional support, crisis intervention, or referrals to further help.

Food & Nutrition Assistance

Groceries, school meals, and community food programs.

Programs that help with food costs and connect people with food banks. Most have income limits or other eligibility rules.

US — United States CONFIRMED

SNAP — Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program

Government food assistance for eligible low-income individuals and families. Benefits help pay for groceries at participating retailers.

Official website: usa.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

WIC — Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children

Nutrition assistance for eligible pregnant, breastfeeding, and postpartum women, infants, and children under five. Provides supplemental food benefits, nutrition education, and healthcare referrals.

Official website: fns.usda.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

National School Lunch Program

Federally assisted school meal program providing nutritious lunches, including free or reduced-price meals for eligible children.

Official website: fns.usda.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

Feeding America

Nonprofit network that helps people locate food banks, food pantries, and other food assistance in their communities.

Official website: feedingamerica.org↗ (external site)

Housing & Homelessness Support

Rental help, public housing, shelter, and homelessness services.

Housing assistance usually depends on income, location, and household size. Local agencies make the decisions, not RedFCU.

USICH — United States Interagency Council on Homelessness

Healthcare & Medical Assistance

Coverage and low-cost care for people who qualify.

Eligibility and covered services differ by state and by program. Community health centers often set fees on a sliding scale based on income.

US — United States CONFIRMED

Medicaid

Government-funded health coverage for eligible people with limited income and resources. Eligibility and coverage vary by state.

Official website: medicaid.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

CHIP — Children's Health Insurance Program

Free or low-cost health coverage for eligible children in qualifying families.

Official website: insurekidsnow.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

HRSA Health Centers

Community health centers providing medical, dental, and mental health services. Participating locations offer fees based on income.

Official website: findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov↗ (external site)

Financial & Utility Assistance

Help with energy bills, income, and benefits.

These are public programs with their own application processes. Some are run by states, so the rules where you live are the ones that apply.

US — United States CONFIRMED

LIHEAP — Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program

Helps eligible households with heating and cooling costs, energy emergencies, and certain energy-related expenses.

Official website: acf.hhs.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

TANF — Temporary Assistance for Needy Families

State-run assistance for eligible families, potentially helping with food, housing, home energy, childcare, and employment-related support.

Official website: usa.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

Unemployment Insurance

Temporary financial benefits for eligible workers who become unemployed through qualifying circumstances.

Official website: dol.gov↗ (external site)
US — United States CONFIRMED

Social Security & SSI

Social Security provides benefits for eligible retirees, people with qualifying disabilities, and survivors. SSI is a separate needs-based program for eligible older adults and people with disabilities or blindness who have limited income and resources.

Official website: ssa.gov↗ (external site)
